## Overview

I've been a swimmer for over nine years, and open water still hands me new
challenges every time. A lot of people never get past the fear of it at all.
XR Safewear is a self-initiated concept for **mixed-reality swim goggles** that
make open water feel navigable and safe, while making the swim itself more
enjoyable.

**How might we** give swimmers an entertaining experience while helping them
feel safe and secure in open water, using mixed reality?

## Research

<Figure
  src="/projects/xr-safewear/01-introduction.jpg"
  alt="Research board covering personal swimming challenges, drowning and water-quality statistics, and existing WaterHCI work"
  caption="Background research across personal experience, swimming safety, and water-focused HCI."
/>

Three threads shaped the problem:

- **Navigation is hard.** Swimming straight and on-route is a persistent
  difficulty when buoys are far apart or the water is dark, and long distances
  get monotonous.
- **Safety is a real barrier.** Drowning is a leading cause of unintentional
  injury death worldwide, and fear of what's in the water (sharks especially)
  keeps people out even when the odds are tiny.
- **The space is mostly built for athletes.** Existing products like FORM's
  smart goggles focus on training and performance, not on reassurance for
  everyday swimmers.

## User study

<Figure
  src="/projects/xr-safewear/02-user-study.jpg"
  alt="Two recreational-swimmer personas and four user-interview summaries, next to a user-journey map and a needs pyramid feeding a prioritized feature list"
  caption="Personas and four interviews, then the swim journey mapped to find where features could earn their place."
/>

I built personas for two audiences, recreational swimmers and
athletes/professionals, and ran four interviews. What came through: people
want navigation help and a connection back to shore, they worry about losing
contact with other people, and current music setups are "complex and tedious to
wear."

## Ideation

Mapping the full journey, from checking conditions to getting out of the water,
surfaced the feature set: **safety warnings, basic swim data, spatial
navigation, seeing other swimmers, and music/entertainment.** The concept sits
at the intersection of mixed reality, underwater audio, and safety.

## Interface

<Figure
  src="/projects/xr-safewear/04-render-and-ui.jpg"
  alt="Product renders of the goggles in several colorways, next to wireframe flows for the heads-up display: home, menu, music, temperature, health, navigation, people nearby, and a shark-nearby danger alert"
  caption="Product renders, and the HUD flows: a glanceable home, a gesture menu, and an escalating danger-alert sequence."
/>

The interface is a heads-up display you control with small hand gestures. A
calm home view shows time and vitals; a radial menu opens navigation, music,
water and air temperature, health, and nearby swimmers. When a hazard is
detected, a **Shark Nearby** alert takes over with a single clear instruction,
_return to shore_, and steps down as the danger passes.
