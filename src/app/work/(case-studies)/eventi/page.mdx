## Overview

**Find events. Make connections. Take a break.** Eventi is a centralized
platform that brings campus and Pittsburgh events together in one place so
students can discover, connect, and actually show up. It was a team of four; I
was **product lead** and owned the app design.

## Problem

<Figure
  src="/projects/eventi/01-problem.jpg"
  alt="Problem statement: students struggle to balance productivity with well-being, event discovery is scattered and inconsistent, and social isolation is a real barrier"
  caption="The problem, in the students' own terms."
/>

Students in demanding programs struggle to balance productivity with
well-being. Socializing takes effort, events are scattered across emails, social
media, and word of mouth, and going alone feels uncomfortable. So valuable
opportunities go unnoticed and people stay in.

## Solution

<Figure
  src="/projects/eventi/02-solution.jpg"
  alt="Solution overview: centralized event discovery, Scotty gamification, and a buddy system, plus a product strategy across five dimensions"
  caption="Three moves (discovery, gamification, and a buddy system), grounded in the technology acceptance model and social capital theory."
/>

Eventi reduces social friction with three moves:

- **Centralized event discovery.** Campus orgs and local hosts in one feed.
- **Scotty gamification.** Quests, achievements, and XP make going out
  rewarding, not just another obligation.
- **A buddy system.** Post an event and find someone to go with.

The strategy piece looks at value-chain positioning, network effects as a moat,
and how the model scales past CMU at low marginal cost.

## The design

<Figure
  src="/projects/eventi/03-the-design.jpg"
  alt="Final Eventi screens: home, activities, avatar and shop, achievements, friends, and calendar"
  caption="The app: a Scotty avatar you dress with earned badges, recommended events, quests, friends, and a calendar."
/>

The mascot, Scotty (CMU's dog), carries the gamification: you earn badges and
items by going to events and dress your avatar with them. Around it sits the
practical stuff: recommendations, a friends view, a buddy finder, and a shared
calendar.
