## Overview

Procrastination and weak planning are near-universal. Opus is a self-initiated
concept for a **gamified planning app** that both helps students organize their
work and motivates them to actually start it. Designed end to end in Figma, with
a short product video.

## Research

<Figure
  src="/projects/opus/01-research.jpg"
  alt="Two student personas, an analysis of goal-setting frameworks, and an information architecture across four core areas"
  caption="Personas, a scan of planning frameworks, and an IA across four core areas."
/>

I built two personas, a high-school student and a university student, and
looked at why planning tools get abandoned. A few principles carried into the
design:

- **Progress tracking motivates.** Visible progress keeps momentum.
- **People are competitive.** Leaderboards and points engage, with quieter
  alternatives for people they don't.
- **Clear, manageable goals keep focus.** SMART goals, Pomodoro, and the
  Eisenhower matrix all point the same way.

That produced an information architecture across four areas: **to-do lists,
community, an AI helper, and personal stats / customization.**

## Wireframes

<Figure
  src="/projects/opus/02-wireframes.jpg"
  alt="Wireframe flows for home, new task, calendar, goal creation (manual and AI-assisted), and the community section"
  caption="Flows for planning (manual and AI-assisted) and for the social layer: forum, friends, rankings, focus rooms."
/>

The core interaction: creating a goal. You can do it manually, or hand a
high-level goal to the **AI helper**, which proposes dailies and to-dos you can
edit. Focus sessions and community "focus rooms" make working feel shared rather
than solitary.

## Visual design

<Figure
  src="/projects/opus/03-visual-design.jpg"
  alt="Final visual design: a dark themed app with a customizable avatar, calendar, AI helper, community feed, and a 3D focus world"
  caption="A dark, playful system: a customizable avatar and pet, points and rewards, and a 3D focus world."

The visual language leans into the game: a customizable avatar and pet, points
and unlockables, and a rendered 3D world for focus sessions. All of it wraps around a
planning tool that stays legible and quick to use.
